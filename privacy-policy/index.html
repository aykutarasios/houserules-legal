Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NextGen Software LLC (“we”, “us”) handles information in the House Rules: Chore Puzzle game for iPhone and Android (“the game”). Cet Tech LLC is listed as the seller on the Apple App Store.

Short version: you do not create an account and we never ask for your name or email. Your progress stays on your device. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, anonymous usage statistics by Google Analytics for Firebase, and purchases are processed by Apple or Google.

1. Who is responsible

NextGen Software LLC, 16192 Coastal Highway, Lewes, Delaware 19958, United States. Email: marketing@nextgensoftlab.com

2. Information the game handles

  • Progress and settings (levels, stars, daily streak, home decorations, sound and language settings, whether ads were removed) are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Family mode player names you type are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. When you tap “Share results”, the text is passed to the share app you choose.
  • Advertising. Google AdMob may process your IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, approximate location inferred from the IP address, and, where you allow it, your device advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android). This is used to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also collect crash and performance diagnostics.
  • Usage statistics. Google Analytics for Firebase processes game events (for example a level started or finished, stars, mistakes, which room was unlocked, ads shown), an app instance identifier and device/app information. We use this only to understand which levels are too hard and to improve the game.
  • Purchases. The optional “Remove ads” purchase is processed by Apple or Google. We receive the confirmation that the product is owned; we never receive payment card or bank details.
  • Support. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write.

The game does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data or precise location, and it does not sell personal information.

3. Your choices

  • Ad consent. In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and US states that require it, the game shows Google’s consent form before ads load. You can play with non-personalised ads.
  • Tracking on iPhone. The game asks for App Tracking Transparency permission. You can change it any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android, manage it in Settings › Google › Ads.
  • No ads. The one-time “Remove ads” purchase turns off full-screen ads. Optional rewarded ads stay available only if you choose to watch them.
  • Delete your data. Deleting the app removes all progress stored on your device. To ask about data processed by Google services, email us.

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

We rely on performing our agreement with you for game features and purchases, your consent for personalised advertising, and our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and improving it for usage statistics and fraud prevention.

5. Service providers and transfers

Google (AdMob, Firebase), Apple and Google Play process information as described above under their own privacy policies: Google, Apple. These providers may process information in the United States and other countries using appropriate safeguards.

6. Retention

Progress stays on your device until you delete the app. Google Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. Support emails are kept as long as needed to answer you.

7. Children

The game is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object, or to withdraw consent. Email us to use these rights. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Important changes will also be shown in the game.
